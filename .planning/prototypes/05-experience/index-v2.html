The role

From the business problem to a frontend that can keep evolving.

I balance practical implementation, architectural judgement and technical coordination—without treating them as separate jobs.

01 / Architecture

Give complex frontends a structure that supports change.

Microfrontends and microapps, shell components, shared libraries, domain decomposition and Angular migrations between major versions.

02 / Data & interface

Make demanding information usable on the client.

Multi-level tables, interdependent calculations, large connected datasets, maps, 2D/3D visualisation and recovery paths for expensive configurations.

03 / Delivery

Own choices through implementation and release.

Technology and integration decisions, implementation, optimisation, tests and participation in CI/CD—not just architecture diagrams.

04 / Collaboration

Coordinate without adding unnecessary layers.

Task distribution and technical coordination for small teams of up to three developers; direct customer communication where it belongs to the role.
