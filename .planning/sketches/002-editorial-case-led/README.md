## Variant: Editorial Case Led

### Design stance
An editorial portfolio that leads with a clear professional point of view, then proves it through two selected stories.

### Key choices
- Layout: reading-first column composition and a lightweight project index.
- Typography: deliberate Fraunces display face + Manrope body, avoiding generic product typography.
- Color: warm paper, clay CTA and mint résumé callout; dark mode remains legible.
- Interaction: theme switch, anchored project navigation, resume download and contact confirmation.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: communicating craft, judgement and depth.
- Weak at: less information is visible above the fold.

### Best for
Hiring managers or clients who value case-study reasoning over a technology checklist.
